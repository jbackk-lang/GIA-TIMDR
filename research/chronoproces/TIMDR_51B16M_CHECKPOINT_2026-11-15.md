## Exact semantics
Controller selector variables represent one (action,next_memory) pair for each (memory,observation), independent of hidden physical state and belief. Reachability variables and unary ranks certify safety of every exact nonterminal belief successor and finite-time current-label diagnosis. Terminate task upon diagnosis. Initial observations all covered. Finite belief universe generated by exact closure under all actions, not controller enumeration.

## Rigorous boundaries
LRAT checker supports RUP additions with explicit unit-propagation hints, not full RAT or deletion rules. Resolution proof generator is naive and expensive; no external industrial SAT solver. No formal proof of implementation correctness; tests are empirical. No general continuous dynamics, immutable-origin tags, canonical model hashing, or infinite postdiagnosis safety. Symbolic coding avoids explicit controller enumeration in the formula, but exponential belief universe and proof size remain possible.
